# Priority brand discount content review

Reviewed 26 October 2026. These are public-source observations, not checkout tests or ranking measurements. Only the four reviewed guide dates were advanced; other brands retain their existing review dates.

The beehiiv, Predis.ai, Taplio and Baremetrics guides now answer the best eligible savings question directly, alongside the existing live coupon status. The answers use provider billing or eligibility options, and do not invent public codes. The existing 30-day freshness rule controls these dated answers and provider savings tables.

| Brand | Reviewed official sources | Content improvement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| beehiiv | [Pricing](https://www.beehiiv.com/pricing), [billing and plan help](https://www.beehiiv.com/support/article/23874462928663-plan-types-and-subscriber-plan-tier-pricing) | Specific promotion redemption steps; monthly pause versus yearly commitment; audience-based plan comparison. |
| Predis.ai | [Pricing](https://predis.ai/pricing/), [auto-renewal and cancellation help](https://help.predis.ai/knowledgebase/en/article/why-was-i-charged-understanding-auto-renewal-on-predis) | Promotional renewal and trial cancellation questions; replaced the old blanket refund note with qualified provider guidance. |
| Taplio | [Discount policy](https://intercom.help/taplio/en/articles/17357648-discounts-and-coupon-codes-on-taplio) | Student/nonprofit availability and rejected-code troubleshooting; direct comparison of eligible savings. |
| Baremetrics | [Pricing](https://baremetrics.com/pricing), [Accelerator](https://baremetrics.com/accelerator) | Annual billing versus coupon guidance; distinguish the current startup directory from older free-startup-plan claims. |

No seasonal sale was added from historical search results. The recorded notes describe what the linked sources published at review time. The catalog's independent offer records and timestamps were not changed by this content review.
