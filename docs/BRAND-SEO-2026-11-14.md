# Individual-brand search improvements — 14 November 2026

This release strengthens the existing canonical `/discounts/<brand>` guides. It does not create duplicate landing pages for every spelling of a query, inflate verification activity, or claim a Google ranking.

## Competitor observations and limits

The public [SimplyCodes Taplio page](https://simplycodes.com/store/taplio.com) has a brand-and-promo-focused title, offer details, source/activity context, redemption guidance and brand FAQs. These are useful patterns for answering a discount search in one place. Its own public FAQ also distinguishes codes on file from verified codes. Those observations do not establish its current Google position, traffic, backlink strength or checkout acceptance.

A controlled REA browser visit on November 14 returned Cloudflare's blocked-page response, not the store. Evidence ID: `ev_57fc303971b717152e657d7e8a877faec14288ceec3d6e4afd02074dfdab83ae`. No challenge was bypassed. The readable store observations above came separately from the web tool's public-page representation; they are not attributed to that blocked browser visit. Competitor wording, screenshots, activity counts and user identities were not copied into SaaSFolio.

Google's [people-first content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) and [structured-data policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/sd-policies) informed the implementation. These are search-quality principles, not a promise of ranking or rich-result eligibility.

## Changes that affect each brand's discoverability

- Restored twelve contextual homepage links to established brand savings guides, including Taplio, beehiiv, Predis.ai and Baremetrics. The A–Z directory now links separately to product profiles and available discount guides. Categories, comparisons and product profiles retain their contextual guide links.
- Moved the actual discount availability answer directly under the brand heading. It distinguishes sourced coupons, provider billing savings and free access, with separate editorial and source-check dates.
- Made the best-value answer consider current coupon terms before static buying advice. A campaign code is not described as a universal saving.
- Added an Article entity for the sourced editorial guide, linked to its canonical WebPage and SoftwareApplication entities. Attribution, modification time and citations correspond to visible page content. No invented Review ratings or merchant prices were added.
- Preserved useful eligibility and troubleshooting sections after a pricing review expires. The fallback consists of questions to verify, without carrying expired numerical savings or old policy assertions into current claims. Expired amounts still leave summaries and FAQs.
- Preserved self-canonicals, query normalization, alias redirects, stable sitemap dates, social images, descriptive links and initial-HTML content. Submission forms remain noindex.

## Verification and ongoing work

The production suite now includes the individual-brand SEO audit, all nine buying-intent checks for all 126 indexed guides, and the new brand-submission browser checks. It also retains sitewide crawl, publisher, browser, accessibility/keyboard, privacy and offer-route checks. Test expectations were updated for the intentionally changed homepage and anonymous brand form; no-JavaScript links and honest unavailable-service behavior remain required.

Use Search Console after deployment to inspect the Taplio URL and sitemap, then monitor actual impressions, clicks and query relevance over time. This repository has no current authenticated Search Console dataset, so it cannot establish a before/after ranking improvement. Fresh content does not by itself produce the independent references and reader trust that a site builds over time.

Online intake still requires the production Supabase connection described in `BRAND-SUBMISSIONS.md`. The GitHub push does not supply missing credentials. Until connected, the form clearly prepares a manual email draft and does not claim to save a database record.

## Additional review findings

The cold-load browser test exposed a mobile headline shift caused by the small introductory label wrapping differently while its font loaded. The label now has a shorter, nonwrapping treatment, and the hero columns align independently. The test continues to require stable heading placement during cold loads and hard refreshes with 4× CPU throttling; its tolerance was not relaxed.

The submission form now declares a POST destination and hides its JavaScript-dependent controls in no-JavaScript mode, where a native email link remains visible. This avoids placing submitted contact details into a browser-generated GET query string. The separate browser tests exercise the real form/API with an isolated temporary store and inspect the native email fallback without sending mail.
