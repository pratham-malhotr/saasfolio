## Source limitations and decisions

All eight providers were operating during review; none was excluded for shutdown. New profile identities are Fathom's AI meeting assistant, tl;dv, Notta and Sembly AI. Fireflies.ai has a separate Free tier. Sembly is classified using its published Free Trial rather than the former Personal-plan model.

The tl;dv `/pricing/` route redirects into `/app/pricing/`, which returned an app shell to text browsing. The guide therefore uses accessible official help for billing choices and nonprofit inquiries, without asserting a public dollar rate, student percentage or universal annual saving. The German help page is linked as the original provider source; the independent buying guidance is in English.

Riverside's pricing HTML contains an old Free card and a staging warning that its information may be outdated. The newer official [trial explanation](https://riverside.com/blog/is-riverside-free), dated 10 September 2026, explicitly describes replacement of the free plan with a paid-plan trial. The guide follows that explicit explanation and flags the conflict. An automated source collector should reject preview/staging pricing content rather than publish its leftover Free card as a current offer.

Descript's former help-center URLs now redirect to its new documentation routes. The current education/nonprofit article was read directly; it publishes the actual approved rate and Hobbyist-equivalent scope, so those details are included without relying on third-party coupon summaries.

Each guide has four original workflow sections totalling at least 280 words, four claim steps, three eligibility checks, two troubleshooting answers and four brand-specific FAQs. Metadata descriptions are below 155 characters. No coupon strings, student percentages, program approvals or successful checkout claims were invented. The shared guide builder controls dated provider observations; the live offer catalog retains independent source records.
