# Research and marketing discount source review

Reviewed on **26 October 2026** for eight original brand guides. This review read public official pricing, help and program pages. No purchase, account signup, education application, professional verification or checkout test was performed. Sources establish published terms, not coupon acceptance for a particular account.

Implementation: `lib/data/research-marketing-discount-guides.ts`, exporting `RESEARCH_MARKETING_DISCOUNT_ENTRIES`. The batch adds four profiles, Ahrefs, Serpstat, SciSpace and Paperguide, and guides for four existing profiles, Semrush, Ranktracker, Elicit and Consensus. Shared integration, catalogs and rendered audits belong to the parent implementation.

## Retrieval limitations and excluded claims

- **Serpstat changed its pricing destination.** Search extraction still surfaced `/page/plans/` with older 40–50% multi-year terms, while opening that URL returned 404. Following the live homepage's Pricing link resolved `/page/pricing-plans/`, which was readable and advertised up to 27%. The new guide uses that current destination and percentage. The program and billing sources also remained readable.
- **SciSpace coupon discovery and collection differed.** The official help was readable through web research and identifies currency-specific yearly codes. The parent's automated source collector received HTTP 403 and preserved the failure. Raw code values are therefore omitted from guide copy and are not represented as current coupon catalog records. The source-linked guide explains where to read the provider's current policy. It does not infer that public coupons do not exist. The parent owns any later evidence capture and actual coupon records.
- **Paperguide displayed a promotion ending on the review date.** The page advertised an extra 50% flash offer through 26 October 2026. Its raw code and percentage were omitted from ongoing guide savings because the promotion may expire before this implementation is published. The standing yearly option is evaluated separately; no stacking is assumed.
- **Consensus pricing was difficult to extract.** The main pricing destination returned minimal readable text, while the official help articles were readable. Eligibility and subscription descriptions rely on those official help pages, without guessing unavailable checkout state.
- **Ahrefs annual language differs between official pages.** The pricing banner says up to 17%, while help uses approximate wording near 20%. The guide follows the current pricing banner and requires the final configuration's invoice. No seasonal coupon is inferred.
- **SciSpace product and usage boundaries matter.** Research and Editor have separate pricing, and Agent usage documents contain changing plan limits. No fixed credit entitlement or blanket unlimited Agent promise is published by the guide.
- No scraped coupon directories, reseller pages, third-party student claims, affiliate commissions or provider testimonials were used to establish discounts. No unverified startup offer was added for any brand.

## Content and query coverage

Each guide answers the brand's discount, coupon/promo availability, annual comparison, eligibility, redemption, troubleshooting and renewal questions on one canonical brand page. Original buying guidance uses the product's actual workflow: verified sites and competitor research for Ahrefs; module credits and course access for Serpstat; toolkit configuration for Semrush; tracking frequency for Ranktracker; research versus Editor and Agent usage for SciSpace; evidence extraction and review seats for Paperguide; academic pricing and export workflows for Elicit; professional verification and research depth for Consensus.

Related links connect the four marketing tools to one another and the four research tools to one another. These are intent and navigation decisions, not measured search volumes or ranking claims. Exact impression improvements still require query-to-page Search Console data after deployment.

All eight entries have four original guidance sections of at least 300 words combined, three buying rows, four claim steps, three eligibility checks, two troubleshooting items, three FAQs and concise brand-specific metadata. Provider observations retain their actual review date and use the existing shared freshness behavior rather than extending old reviews automatically.
