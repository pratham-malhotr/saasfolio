## Coupon-publisher crawling

Eight public discovery sources were added: four WPBeginner pages, two Wethrift pages and two SimplyCodes pages. The collector respects robots rules, one-second same-host spacing, bounded public HTTPS responses and backoff. It pauses a host after an access-denial response. In this run, publisher requests were blocked or skipped following the host denial. **No publisher code was imported from those failures.** The research links remain visible with attribution and the limitation.

Additional exploratory requests to WPLift were blocked; a guessed Themeisle article returned 404 and was not added to the source registry. A nonexistent Text Blaze `/pricing/` path was replaced with the actual `/plans/` page. SocialPilot's older `/plans` response lacked usable content; its working `/pricing` endpoint supplies the current evidence.

Direct automated access to Perfmatters and Scholarcy returned 403. Their guides use separately readable official research without converting the failed requests into successful offer checks. TypingMind returned insufficient visible content, so its promotion was withheld. Missing dynamic prices and ambiguous discounts are not reconstructed.

Paperpal's public trial invitation was scoped to institution-wide sales enquiries. The discovery matcher now rejects that wording as evidence of an unrestricted individual trial, and a separately reviewed institutional listing preserves the actual audience. A regression test checks both that exclusion and ordinary individual trials. Existing source timestamps, content fingerprints, failures and retired URL identity remain intact.

The [source report](reports/brand-expansion-sources-2026-10-28.json) records each brand, new-profile status, official source, published offer identities and source outcomes. A source check is not successful redemption, provider approval or independent verification of product performance.
